Talktu lives in your menu bar and turns speech into clean, ready-to-send text in whatever app
you're using: Slack, Mail, your editor, a terminal, anywhere you can type. Hold the hotkey, talk,
let go. That's it.

## Features

### ⚡ Insistence on performance

Dictation is only useful if it's faster than typing, so Talktu treats every millisecond as a
bug. It's ready the instant you press the key, it catches your very first word, and your text
lands the moment you let go. Nothing leaves your Mac, so there's no server to wait on.

### 🔒 Private by design

Everything happens on your Mac. No accounts, no cloud, and your voice is never recorded to disk.

### ✨ Text you'd actually send

Raw speech is messy. Talktu tidies it up before it's pasted: filler words gone, punctuation and
capitalization fixed, and rambling sentences turned into something you'd actually write.

### 👀 Spells what's on your screen

Names, project jargon, and handles visible in your current window come out spelled right, even
when you say them quickly.

### 📝 Your vocabulary, spelled right

Add your own names and terms in Settings and Talktu will get them right every time.

### 🎛️ Tuned to your Mac

Choose between snappier or more accurate, and dictate in more than one language.

### 🤫 Stays out of your way

- **Hold to talk**, or **tap to toggle** for longer thoughts.
- Press **Escape** to throw a dictation away.
- Your clipboard is left exactly as it was.
- A small overlay shows what's happening, then gets out of sight.
- Recent dictations are kept in **history**, so you can reuse something you said earlier.

## Install

1. Download the `.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Talktu** into **Applications**.
3. Launch it and grant the permissions it asks for: Microphone, Accessibility, and Input
   Monitoring. Screen Recording is optional.

Every build is signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, so it opens without
any Gatekeeper workarounds.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- A Mac with Apple Silicon
